Vehicle Storage

Secure, covered storage in Bradford, short or long term, with recovery to the door.

Secure, covered vehicle storage in Bradford

Whether you're away for a few weeks, waiting on an insurance decision or simply need to free up space, we can store your vehicle safely in our covered facility. And because we're a recovery company, we can collect it and deliver it back to you.

Why store with us

  • Protection from the elements: indoor, covered storage out of the rain, sun and snow
  • Security: a secure site that reduces the risk of theft and vandalism
  • Convenience: collection and delivery by our own recovery trucks
  • Flexibility: short or longer-term arrangements to suit you

Storage after an accident

If your vehicle has been in an accident, we can recover it directly into storage and keep it safe while your insurer assesses it. A number of our customers have used us this way.

For availability and pricing, call us or send a message and we'll get back to you.
