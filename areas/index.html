Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you get to me?

In many cases we can be with you inside 30 minutes. Arrival times depend on where you are and how busy we are, and we will give you an honest estimate when you call.

Are you really open 24 hours a day?

Yes. Our recovery line is answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

What vehicles can you recover?

Everything from motorbikes through cars, 4x4s, MPVs and vans up to heavy goods vehicles. If you are unsure, just call and ask.

Can I travel in the truck with my vehicle?

Yes. You can ride in the cab with our driver, and we will take you and your vehicle to your home, a garage or your workplace.

Can you store my vehicle after an accident?

Yes. We can recover your vehicle straight into covered storage and keep it there while your insurer decides what happens next.

Are you insured?

Yes, we are fully insured.
